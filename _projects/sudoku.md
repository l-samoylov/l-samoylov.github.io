## Overview
How fast can you solve sudoku? 

By [representing sudoku as a satisfiability (SAT) problem](https://www.eeecs.qub.ac.uk/~I.Spence/SuDoku/SuDoku.html), where you want each row/square/column to obey certain rules, you can solve it with sat solvers! This project compares WalkSAT, GSat, and the Davis–Putnam–Logemann–Loveland (DPLL) algorithm for SAT solving. [See here in Github](https://github.com/l-samoylov/SudokuSolvers)
