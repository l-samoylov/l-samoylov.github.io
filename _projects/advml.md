## Overview
Poupourri of Topics: 

In Part 1) I implemented core components of large language models from scratch, including transformer architectures, automatic differentiation, efficient inference, distributed training, and reinforcement learning for mathematical reasoning
## Topics Covered 
- **LLM Inference**  Implemented byte-pair encoding (BPE), rotary positional embeddings (RoPE), KV caching, and batched autoregressive inference 

- **Distributed Training & RL** Built distributed supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning pipelines using CUDA/NCCL, training language models on mathematical reasoning tasks with group-normalized policy gradients

- **Automatic Differentiation & Transformers** Implemented a reverse-mode autodifferentiation framework using CuPy, including custom gradients and components of a decoder-only transformer
